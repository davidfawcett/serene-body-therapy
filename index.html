About Us

Serene Body Therapy was started by Care in 2022, with a deep commitment to therapeutic massage. Initially working out of rented space in Legacy Health and Fitness gym, her customer focus and expertise soon created a loyal client base. After much searching and waiting she was able to find a better shop front at 90a Maroondah Hwy and moved her practice there.

Joyfully, Care became pregnant and continued to provide exceptional care to her clients throughout her pregnancy but also struggled to run the business alone. Jasmine another expert in Thai massage joined her to help manage the practice and ensure that all clients received the highest quality care.

After the birth of her daughter Care decided to step away from the business and focus on her family, Jasmine was able to reincorporate the business and continue providing excellent service to all clients.

We are very grateful to all the excellent work Care has done and we look forward to welcoming her back to Serene Body Therapy when she feels the time is right.
